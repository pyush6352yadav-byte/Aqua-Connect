    How It Works

    A student's journey with AquaConnect – from finding water to getting a problem fixed.

    About AquaConnect

    Making campus water management smarter and more sustainable.

    01 Who We Are

    AquaConnect is a smart campus water-management website built by students, for students. It helps everyone on campus find working drinking water, dispose of plastic bottles responsibly, and report problems that college management can fix quickly.

    02 The Problem

    Everyday water problems on campus often go unnoticed or unsolved:

    🚰 Hard to find waterStudents don't know which coolers are working, so they walk from block to block.
    🍾 Bottle wasteEmpty plastic bottles are left around, and bins overflow without anyone being alerted.
    💦 Unreported faultsLeaks, dirty water and damaged coolers have no easy way to be reported or tracked.

    03 Our Mission & Vision

    🎯 MissionTo give every student quick access to clean drinking water and a simple way to report problems and recycle responsibly.
    🌍 VisionA campus where no water is wasted, no bottle is littered, and every issue is solved fast.

    04 What AquaConnect Offers

    🛡️ Human Verification"I'm not a robot" check before the website opens.
    📍 Water Point LocatorFind nearby drinking water facilities.
    🔴 Live StatusWorking, Not Working or Under Maintenance.
    📱 QR Code ReportingScan a QR code on a cooler to report problems.
    🧭 Smart Route SuggestionsNearest available point from your block.
    ♻️ Bottle Recycling LocatorFind the nearest plastic bottle collection bin.
    📸 Empty Bottle PhotoUpload a photo, tag its location and get routed to the nearest bin.
    🌱 Eco PointsPoints for responsible bottle recycling.
    🗺️ Availability HeatmapSee where working water points are scarce.
    👥 Crowd-Based UpdatesStudents confirm whether a point is working.
    ⭐ Rating & FeedbackRate facilities and share water-quality feedback.
    🆔 Complaint TrackingDigital complaint ID with Pending, In Progress and Resolved status.
    🔔 Smart NotificationsGet notified when your reported problem is resolved.
    ⚠️ Maintenance PriorityUrgent issues get higher priority.
    🧑‍💼 Admin DashboardCollege management can manage complaints, bins and bottle reports.
    💡 Water Saving TipsSuggestions for avoiding water wastage.

    05 Our Impact

    ♿ AccessibilityClean drinking water is easy to find for everyone, from any block, on any device.
    🌿 SustainabilityBetter bottle recycling means less plastic waste on campus.
    💧 Responsible water consumptionFaster leak reports and saving tips reduce water wastage.
